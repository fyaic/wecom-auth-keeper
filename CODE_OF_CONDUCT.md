# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, discuss technical ideas with evidence, and assume good intent when asking for clarification.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, enterprise data or private conversations in community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and context.

To report a concern, use GitHub's reporting tools or a private contact method explicitly offered by a repository maintainer. Do not publish personal details in a public issue to make a report.
